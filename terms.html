13.Data Backup and Availability

This version of the App keeps your data on your device and nowhere else. That makes your own export the only backup there is, so take one regularly (Settings > Export Data) and keep the file somewhere you control.

Know what the export contains before you rely on it. It carries the records you enter — aircraft, components, maintenance tasks, flight, maintenance and defect logs, the parts shelf, and notes. It does not carry your suppliers, your quality reports, your aircraft cover photos, your reminder preferences, comments and assigned tasks, or your saved settings including the signature used on log cards. Photo and document rows travel in the file but the image files themselves do not. If those matter to you, keep a device backup as well.
