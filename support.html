What Mareo Is

Mareo is a reminder and tracking app. You enter your aircraft, their components, the intervals you follow and the hours you fly; the app keeps the countdowns, shows what is overdue and what is coming due, and reminds you before a date arrives.

It is not an official authority, a regulator or a certifying body, and it issues no approvals or official determinations. Every due date, interval and life limit in it came from you, and has to be checked by you against the official documentation and a qualified technician. Never skip or delay an inspection because the app did or did not remind you — a reminder's presence, absence or timing decides nothing.

Where Your Records Live

This version of Mareo runs entirely on your device. There is no account to create, no sign-in, and no server. The records you enter are written to storage on this device and are never sent to us or to anyone else.

That has a consequence worth stating plainly rather than leaving to be discovered: we never receive your records, so we cannot read them, correct them, produce them on request, or put them back if they are gone. There would be nothing on our side to look up. It also means losing the device loses the records — your own export is the only copy that survives it.

Reminders are scheduled by iOS on this device. Nothing is registered with any server, and no push token is created.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new tracker item?

From the main screen, tap the "+" button and enter the details of the item you want to track.

How do reminders work?

Mareo tracks your schedules and timelines, then notifies you when items are due or approaching.

Where is my data stored?

On this device, and nowhere else. There is no account and no server: your records are written to this device's storage and are never sent anywhere. That also means losing the device loses the data, so take an export from Settings and keep it somewhere safe.

How do I export or back up my data?

Go to Settings and tap Export Data. On this version that file is your only backup — nothing is stored anywhere else — so take one regularly and keep it somewhere you control.

Do my records move to a new device?

Not on their own. Your records live on this device only, so moving to a new phone means moving the file yourself: go to Settings, tap Export Data, keep the file somewhere you control, then restore it from Settings on the new device. Syncing between devices is something we are working on.

Can I share this with my team?

Not yet. This version is built for one person on one device — there are no accounts and no shared access. If you need to hand your records to someone else, export the file and send it to them; they can restore it in their own copy of the app. Shared access is something we are working on.

Is Mareo an official authority or professional service?

No. Mareo is an organizational app for reminders and tracking only. It does not provide official determinations or professional advice.

Backing Up and Moving Your Records

Settings > Export Data writes everything the file can hold into a single JSON file and hands it to the iOS share sheet, so you can save it wherever you keep your own files. Settings > Restore from Backup reads one back. Those two controls are the whole of it: there is no copy anywhere else, and no one to ask for one.

Know what the export contains before you rely on it. It carries the records you enter — aircraft, components, maintenance tasks, flight, maintenance and defect logs, the parts shelf, and notes. What it does NOT put back is: aircraft cover photos; your notification history; the photo and document files themselves; and the record of every change you saved. Two more travel in the file and are not applied on restore: the record of your photos and documents; and the activity history, with its comments and tasks. If those matter to you, keep a device backup as well.

One thing about the file itself: it is plain readable text, not encrypted. That is deliberate — it means you can open it, check it, and restore it anywhere without depending on us — but it also means anyone who gets the file can read everything in it. Keep it somewhere you control, and think before sending it to anyone, including us.

If Reminders Are Not Arriving

Reminders are delivered by iOS, and iOS delivers them only if you have allowed notifications for Mareo. If nothing is arriving, there are two places to look. First, the iOS Settings app, under Notifications, where permission is granted per app — until it is allowed there, none of these arrive. Second, Mareo's own Settings screen, where the Notifications card carries Maintenance Reminders, Component Alerts, System Notifications, Reminder Frequency, Days Before Due, Reminder Time and Quiet Hours; a reminder can be switched off, scheduled further ahead, or delayed into quiet hours from there.

If reminders arrive but not for the item you expected, the item itself is the next place to check: reminders are generated only from what you have entered, so an item with no interval and no due date has nothing to remind you about.

We do not guarantee that any reminder will be generated, delivered or on time. Device settings and operating-system behaviour outside our control can delay or prevent delivery, which is the reason the app is a tracking aid and not something to decide by.
